About

I am a PhD student at Imperial College London. My research combines machine learning with physical modelling to build differentiable tools for understanding the atmospheres of planets beyond our Solar System from JWST observations.

Before starting my PhD, I spent a year at the HomanLab within the Psychiatric University Hospital of Zürich, combining brain imaging and large language models to understand how the brain processes narratives. I studied at ETH Zürich, where my master's thesis explored physics-informed neural networks for seismology.
