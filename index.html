Davide Staub

PhD student, Imperial College London

I work in the Scalable Scientific Machine Learning Lab with Dr Ben Moseley, developing physics-based, differentiable methods for analysing JWST observations of transiting exoplanets.

Before the PhD, I spent a year at the HomanLab at the Psychiatric University Hospital of Zürich, using brain imaging and large language models to study how people follow stories. I studied at ETH Zürich, where my master's thesis was on physics-informed neural networks for seismology.
