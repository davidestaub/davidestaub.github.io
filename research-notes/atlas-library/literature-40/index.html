Coverage and reading status

This library combines NASA Exoplanet Archive references, arXiv searches and targeted literature searches. The bulk searches were saved on 10 September 2026, with targeted checks on 13 and 14 September. Coverage is incomplete, and many records await a full-text review.

Each citation in a planet’s entry links to the section it supports. Other indexed records await individual assessment. A host-star search can also find studies of its other planets, the star itself or a comparison sample. Topic labels come from titles and abstracts.

Records with matching arXiv IDs or supplied DOIs are combined. Preprint and journal versions can still appear separately if they lack a shared identifier. Publication and reading status are shown for each record; DOI registration alone does not establish peer review.

Expanded searches supplement the earlier TRAPPIST-1 results. The search log records which queries were completed. Papers can still be missed if they use different names or are absent from the indexes. Some full texts are inaccessible.
