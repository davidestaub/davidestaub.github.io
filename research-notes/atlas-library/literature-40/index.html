Coverage and reading status

This is a literature inventory, not a claim that every publication has been found or read. It combines NASA Exoplanet Archive references, saved arXiv searches and targeted literature searches. The saved bulk searches date from 10 September 2026. Targeted checks were made on 13 September; an attempted arXiv bulk refresh returned a rate-limit error.

“Cited on site” identifies sources linked to a specific part of a planet’s entry. “Indexed” records are search matches awaiting individual assessment. A paper matching the host star may concern another planet, the star itself or a comparison sample. Topic tags are search aids based on titles and abstracts.

Matching arXiv IDs and supplied DOIs are merged. References lacking shared identifiers can remain separate records, including preprint and journal versions. A DOI does not by itself certify peer review. These counts are bibliographic records, not independently reviewed papers.

The earlier capped TRAPPIST-1 host search is supplemented by expanded per-planet searches. Search completion refers only to the recorded query, not to all literature about the system. Papers that omit the searched names, inaccessible full texts, and publications missing from the indexes remain coverage gaps.
