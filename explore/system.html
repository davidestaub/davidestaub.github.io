Catalogue: NASA Exoplanet Archive (PSCompPars). Sky: HYG database. Rendering assumptions are listed under "imagined".

Notes

What is real. The star's radius, temperature and mass, and each planet's radius, mass, orbital period, semi-major axis and eccentricity come from the catalogue where they exist. Where one of a pair is missing it is derived from the other and marked so. The scene is at true scale: one scene unit is 1,000 km, so a hot Jupiter sits a few stellar radii out and a one-year orbit is a large empty circle. The background stars are the bright-star catalogue moved to the host's position.

What is a model. No exoplanet has ever been resolved as a disc. Surface pattern, colour, cloud cover, spin, the orientation of the orbit and the starting position along it are all chosen by the renderer from rules based on the planet's class and equilibrium temperature. Those rules are stated in the dossier; treat them as an illustration, not as a prediction.
