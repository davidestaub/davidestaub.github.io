Reading the air of other worlds

Humanity has confirmed more than 6,000 planets around other stars, and with JWST we can now study their air from hundreds of light-years away. A hands-on tour of transmission spectroscopy: fly a planet across its star, watch the starlight dip, and build a spectrum yourself.
